# Admins book Spaces for Guests

The first version had no guests: a Guest's parking was arranged outside the app. Since
2026-09-29 Admins book Spaces for Guests in the app, because a Space promised to a Guest
outside the app is one the app can still hand to a colleague.

A Guest Booking is an ordinary Booking with the Guest's name on it, held by the Admin who
made it. So it gets its Space the way any Booking does (ADR-0001), frees its time to the
Waitlist when cancelled, and moves or goes when its Space is Blocked or its day is closed,
with that Admin told. It goes too if that Admin is deactivated.

The rules that ration Spaces among colleagues don't apply to it. Admins make as many as they
need, on any open day from today on, past the Booking Window, with no Booking Limit and no
one-a-day rule, and none of it counts against their own Bookings.

What a Guest Booking can't do is take a Space someone already holds: it gets a free Space or
nothing, and a Guest never waits on the Waitlist. To make room for a Guest on a full day, an
Admin has to ask a colleague to cancel, or cancel a Booking themselves, which tells that
colleague.
