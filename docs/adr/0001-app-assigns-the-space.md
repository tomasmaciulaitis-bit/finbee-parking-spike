# The app assigns the Space; Colleagues don't pick one

A Colleague books "a Space" for a day and period, and the app chooses which Space the
Booking gets. It fits Part-Day Bookings together so that whole Spaces stay free for
Whole-Day Bookings. The number is shown at once and never changes afterwards, with one
exception: when an Admin Blocks the Space, the app moves the Booking to another free one.

We chose this over letting Colleagues pick, which most parking apps do, because with 7
shared Spaces and more demand than supply, picking scatters Part-Day Bookings across
Spaces. On a busy day that can leave no Space free for a whole day even though the
capacity exists, and rationing that capacity is the point of the app. It also lets the
waitlist wait for any Space rather than a particular one.

This relies on the Shared Spaces being interchangeable. If tandem Spaces (one car blocking
another), EV chargers or otherwise different Spaces appear, revisit this decision.
