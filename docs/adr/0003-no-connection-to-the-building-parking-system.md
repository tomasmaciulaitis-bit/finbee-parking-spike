# The app is not connected to the building's parking system

The garage entrance admits cars by a number-plate camera that the building runs. The app
does not connect to that system in any way: it neither sends plates to it nor produces
plate lists for anyone to send. Which plates the camera admits is arranged with building
management outside the app, and the Number Plates in the app only tell colleagues whose
car is whose.

This holds only while the building admits every finbee driver's car and leaves the
question of who parks where to finbee. If the building ever limits finbee to a fixed set
of plates (one per Space), the camera would have to follow each day's Bookings, which
Part-Day Bookings and late Promotions make impossible to do by hand. Revisit this decision
then.
